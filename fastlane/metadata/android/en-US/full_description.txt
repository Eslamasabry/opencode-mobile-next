OpenCode Mobile is an unofficial, independent community app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the OpenCode team, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. It is free, open source (MIT), and written by one developer with substantial AI assistance.

Run a coding agent on your Android phone, or follow the one on your computer. Ask for a change, watch it work, approve what it wants to do, and review the result.

<b>On this phone</b>
• The app installs its own Ubuntu with Git, Python and Node.js. No computer, no Termux. The first setup downloads about 210 MB (about 4 minutes), checks each step, and resumes where it left off.
• Ten agents, picked per conversation: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Qwen Code, Kilo, Cline, Goose, Oh My Pi, fx and OpenCode. Claude Code and OpenCode are the best proven on phones; the others are newer and less tested. Each agent needs its own provider sign-in or key. OpenCode's free models need none.
• Needs a 64-bit phone. Phones with about 2 GB of memory work, slowly.
• See what is making the phone hot, and stop programs left behind.

<b>On your computer</b>
• Connect to OpenCode 2 by QR code or address. Tailscale lets you reach it from anywhere.
• Claude Code and Codex also work through a Paseo helper on your own network.

<b>Chat that keeps up</b>
• Replies stream as they are written. One live line says what the agent is doing, with one Stop always in the same place.
• Reads, edits and commands fold into one line you can open.
• Approve or reject each request before it acts. Diffs fit the phone's width.
• Git in the chat: tap the branch, tick files, save and send to GitHub.
• Talk instead of type: voice is transcribed on the phone and never leaves it.

<b>Away from the app</b>
• Two home-screen widgets, Conversations and Status, put what needs you first.
• Inbox: everything that waits on you across servers, in one list.
• Allow or reject from a notification.

<b>Make it yours</b>
• Appearance asks one question at a time: light or dark, colours, one of five styles, text size. You start on Solid with the moving background off.
• English and Arabic, with partial Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, Portuguese and Russian. Large text up to 200%.

<b>Your data</b>
The app talks only to the servers you choose. Passwords stay in Android's keystore. No ads, no analytics, no automatic crash upload: a report is sent only when you send one, with secrets removed. You bring your own model provider.
