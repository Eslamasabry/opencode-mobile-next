# Glass audit, 2026-11-11 (auditor only: nothing changed, nothing committed)

Source read: `~/Storage2/wt/liquid-merge`, branch tip `291e64b56d`, app under `native/`. All paths below are relative to `native/` unless they start with `/`.
Yardstick: `~/.claude/skills/liquid-glass/` (SKILL.md, references/audit.md, references/recipes.md). Rules are quoted as "Skill: ...".

## Verdict

"Is this supposed to be glass?" Yes, the code does draw glass on the home screen, but the picture the owner sees is the skill's first failure: **glass over a near-black page is a dark rectangle with a rim** (Skill: "Something behind it"). Three things stack up. (1) The page is almost flat: the Glass background tints the ground with only 14 % of the aurora's colour in dark (`ui/design/.../effects/AuroraImage.kt:35`, "toned down" on 2026-10-26), and the owner's phone caught the dimmest part of its drift. (2) The in-flow panes (list panel, chips, "All conversations") are frost with no lens by design, and their face is a 16 % tint plus a sheen at the top edge only; the rim at the bottom end is almost gone (alpha .07, `ui/kit/.../effects/KitGlassPanes.kt:90`). (3) Only the chat screen has chrome floating over scrolling content; every other screen has a flat bar above a body that stops under it, and the home screen has no bar at all, so the one thing that makes liquid glass look like liquid glass (content passing under a lens) never happens there. The fix order is: the page first, then the pane face, then floating bars on every page. All three are shared-kit changes.

## Top five findings (details further down)

1. **DESIGN, kit-wide.** The page behind Glass has no shape: dark chroma 14 %, a slow drift, so most of the time it is near-black (home-dark-owner-phone.png vs home-dark-emulator-bright-aurora.png).
2. **DESIGN, kit-wide.** Only the chat has a floating liquid bar and body running under it; about 100 other screens (home, settings, files, git, terminal, servers, agents, search) have chrome beside the content, so no lens ever shows.
3. **DESIGN, kit-wide.** An in-flow pane cannot stand on its own in the dark: tint .16, sheen at the top edge only, bottom-end rim .07 (dark) / top rim white on a near-white page (light).
4. **BUG, one place.** Markdown markers (`**http://127.0.0.1:8150/**`, back-ticks, emoji) show raw in the home list's preview line.
5. **BUG, kit-wide.** Toast and Undo bar are plain solid `surface3` / `surface2` boxes under Glass; they float over content and should be glass.

## Findings, ranked

Ranking: how often the owner sees the screen, how flat it reads, then how shared the fix is. Shared-kit fixes come first.

### DESIGN CHOICES (the owner picks)

**D1. The page gives glass nothing to show (every Glass screen, dark worst).**
- What is wrong: the Glass page is the ground plus a painted aurora shown as a tint at 14 % chroma in dark and 12 % in light (`AuroraImage.kt:34-35`), drifting slowly with sine periods of minutes (`AuroraImage.kt:116-121`). The owner's phone caught a dim moment; the emulator at another moment shows a clear green wash (compare the two home pictures). Over most of the screen the pane sits on a flat page.
- Rule: Skill "What makes glass read as glass" 1: "Over a flat or near-black page a perfect pane is a dark rectangle with a rim... the fix is usually behind the pane (content that scrolls under it, a picture, a glow with shape)". Skill recipes "Dark pages": "A background picture or glow with shape and a little colour".
- Fix: give the page shape that is always there at the places panes sit: raise dark chroma, and/or pin one or two soft colour blobs (position not left to drift alone) behind the first panel and the chip row. Owner picks between "stronger aurora", "pinned blobs" and "a picture". Mock these on the real home screen first (one phone screen per image).
- Scope: kit-wide, one place. Files: `ui/design/src/commonMain/.../effects/AuroraImage.kt`, `ui/kit/src/commonMain/.../live/KitLiveBackground.kt`, `ui/design/.../style/GlassSpec.kt` (the `live` policy, line 20).
- Cost to watch: panes thicken until words read over the brightest spot of the picture (`GlassFinish.thickened`, `KitSurface.kt` around the `spots` list), so a brighter page makes panes thicker unless the knee/ink rules from the skill are used.

**D2. Chrome sits beside content, not over it, on every page except the chat.**
- What is wrong: home has no bar (`ChatsScreen.kt:229`); Settings, Files, Git, Terminal, Servers, Agents, Search have a bar in a column above a body that stops at it. A body runs under a liquid bar only when it records itself (chat) or has a `header` slot (`KitScreen.kt:480-487`). So no lens ever has content to bend.
- Rule: Skill audit.md section 3: "Chrome that sits beside content instead of over it (a header above a list, not floating over it), so the lens never shows. Edge-to-edge content under a floating bar is where liquid glass earns its place."
- Fix: make "body runs under a floating liquid bar" the default for every `KitScreen` that has a top bar (home: turn the title / search / gear row into the floating bar over the list). Open choices: all pages, or only pages long enough to scroll; the glass-on-short-pages case shows nothing until scrolled.
- Scope: kit-wide (`KitScreen`), then home needs its own small change. Files: `ui/kit/.../frame/KitScreen.kt` (lines 255-345, 474-545), `ui/kit/.../frame/KitTopEdge.kt`, `feature/chats/.../ui/ChatsScreen.kt:226-245`, `feature/settings/.../ui/SettingsScreen.kt:351`. Single-owner units per AGENTS.md: `KitScreen.kt` and the chat screen files are separate owners.
- Cost: the window's liquid budget is 5 (`LiquidEdge.BUDGET`, `LiquidEdge.kt` about line 70); one more recorded layer per page; this is the biggest build of the three.

**D3. In-flow panes cannot stand on their own face in the dark, and have no light at the bottom end.**
- What is wrong: a clear Content pane is a 16 % tint (dark, `GlassSpec.kt:112`) with a sheen near the top edge; the rim is .24 at the top start and .07 at the bottom end in dark (`KitGlassPanes.kt:88-91`), .60 / .16 in light, white on a near-white page, so it does not show. The pane reads as "slightly lighter dark box" (home-dark-owner-phone.png, panel at y 410-1190).
- Rule: Skill "One light": "lit where the edge faces the top start and the bottom end". Recipes "Dark pages": "a face that stands on its own: a slightly lighter fill than the page, a sheen along the top edge, a firmer rim".
- Fix: add the bottom-end light (a thin lit segment, not a frame), a firmer rim, and in light a darker hairline (shade, not white) so the pane's edge shows on a white page. No halo.
- Scope: kit-wide, small. Files: `ui/design/.../style/GlassSpec.kt` (rims, around line 322), `ui/design/.../style/GlassLook.kt:46-53` (look numbers), `ui/kit/.../effects/KitGlassPanes.kt:70-95`.

**D4. Wasted lens on the + button; chips over nothing (home).**
- What is wrong: the + is a liquid "drop" (`KitFab.kt:136`, tint .55 dark, `LiquidEdge.kt` Action recipe) but sits on the blank part of the page, so it reads as a flat green disc with a rim (owner photo, lower right). The three chips are frost on an empty strip.
- Rule: Skill audit.md section 2: "Does content ever pass under it? If not, a lens there is wasted; frost or a self-standing face is enough."
- Fix: solved mostly by D1/D2 (content under it). If the owner wants nothing under the + , give it a sheen and a rim and stop paying for the lens there. Choose after D2.
- Scope: single part. Files: `ui/kit/.../action/KitFab.kt`.

**D5. Rows with no pane at all (Files).**
- What is wrong: `FolderScreen.kt` has 9 `KitRow`s and no `KitRowGroup`, so file rows sit directly on the page while other screens group them in frost panels (mixed treatment between screens).
- Rule: Skill audit.md section 3: "Mixed materials on one screen"; the table says list panels are frost.
- Fix: decide per screen whether long file lists should be one frost panel or stay open on the page (an open list under a floating bar is also a good glass setting, see D2). Check on a phone before building.
- Scope: single screen. Files: `feature/files/src/commonMain/.../ui/FolderScreen.kt`.

### BUGS (rule broken or plainly wrong)

**B1. Raw markdown markers in the home list's preview line.**
- Seen: `Made you something. **http://127.0.0.1:8150/** A proce...`, `**PC Health Check** ✅ **System** ...`, back-ticked paths (home-dark-owner-phone.png, rows 1, 2, 3, 4).
- Cause: `HomePreviewRules.oneLine` only folds whitespace (`feature/chats/src/commonMain/.../presentation/HomePreviews.kt:31-35`); the screen passes it straight on (`feature/chats/.../ui/ChatsScreen.kt:786-788`). The row cannot show bold, and the glass panel makes the noise louder.
- Rule: Skill audit.md section 3: "Raw or noisy content under glass (markdown markers, long URLs)". Also `docs/parity/MARKDOWN-EVAL.md` exists for the same area.
- Fix: strip inline markers (`**`, `__`, back-ticks, link syntax, heading marks) when building the preview; shorten bare URLs to the host; keep emoji. Add a test in the chats module (a failing test first).
- Scope: single screen's data path (shared with All conversations, same `HomePreviewRules`).

**B2. Toast and Undo bar are solid boxes under Glass.**
- `ui/kit/.../modal/KitToast.kt:124-125` (`clip(shape).background(colors.surface3)`) and `KitUndoBar.kt:128-129` (`surface2`). They float over content.
- Rule: Skill audit.md section 1: "toasts ... Floats over content: Liquid"; Skill "No per-screen glass: a missing pane goes into the shared kit" (it is already in the kit, just missing the Glass branch). `LiquidRole.Status` (a small calm pill) already exists for this.
- Fix: give both a Glass face (Status/Pill recipe: blur 1, refraction 12, tint .18 dark / .28 light), keep the solid fill for every other style and for the held states.
- Scope: kit-wide, small. Files: `ui/kit/.../modal/KitToast.kt`, `KitUndoBar.kt`, `ui/kit/.../effects/KitLiquidGlass.kt`.

**B3. When the picture is missing, Glass falls back to the old thick panes with a drop-shadow halo.**
- Cause: `kitGlassBlurs` is false when the backdrop is not painted (Background off, Saver, reduced motion, high contrast, below Android 12, every desktop render). Then panes use `opacity()` (.42 / .50 Content) and `shadows` with a 32 dp blur (`GlassSpec.kt:246-256, 280`): see home-light-desktop-held.png (white cards with shadows).
- Rule: Skill "Shadow: none ... crisp edges"; Skill "What not to do": decorations that read as a card.
- Fix: for the fallback, draw the same crisp face without the halo; or state that the fallback is intended and keep it out of the Glass look. Owner decides if the fallback should look like Glass or like Soft.
- Side effect for the team: **every Glass golden in the repo is this fallback**, so none of them can show whether glass reads as glass. Real judging needs a phone or an emulator frame with the aurora painted.
- Scope: kit-wide. Files: `ui/design/.../style/GlassSpec.kt`, `ui/kit/.../foundation/KitSurface.kt:138-230`.

**B4. Dividers inside the panel are heavy and run into the rim.**
- Seen: the home panel's row lines are at least 1 dp, ink at 30 % (`ui/kit/.../foundation/KitDivider.kt:85-100`), requested by `prominentDividers = true` (`ChatsScreen.kt:388, 400`), and the line reaches the pane's right edge (home-dark-owner-phone.png at x 865).
- Rule: Skill audit.md section 3: "Decorations that fight the glass: ... heavy dividers inside a pane".
- Fix: under Glass use a short, light hairline inset from both ends (start on the words' edge, end one gutter before the rim), not the prominent rule; keep prominent for Solid where it was asked (B-173).
- Scope: kit-wide. Files: `ui/kit/.../row/KitRecentRow.kt:300-331`, `ui/kit/.../row/KitRowGroup.kt:124`, `KitDivider.kt`.

**B5. Chat chips are calm glass over a dark transcript.** Not a bug in code (the recipe is the skill's pill), but in chat-dark-emulator.png the chips ("Ask first", "build - Model") read as outlined dark pills: same page problem as D1. No separate fix.
