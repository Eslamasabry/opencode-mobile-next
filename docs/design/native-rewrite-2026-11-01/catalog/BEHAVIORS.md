### Level meaning (`native_level`)

- `unit`: pure logic in one class, no I/O.
- `contract`: wire or native-bridge contract checked against recorded payloads or fixtures.
- `integration`: several real parts together with fakes at the edges (fake server, fake clock, fake disk).
- `screenshot`: a rendered screen or part checked by a screenshot or semantics test.
- `migration`: stored format or one-shot upgrade that must keep reading old data.
- `e2e`: only meaningful on a running app and device.
