## What changed, per part

### part-kit_row_parts (KitSwitchRow, KitExpandRow, KitRowParts)

- **KitSwitchRow** (`kit_row_parts.dart:279-589`):
  - Solid keeps the platform switch, as Flutter's `Switch.adaptive` under `app_theme.dart:457-478`: 60 × 48 box,
    52 × 32 track, a 2 dp hairline round an off track, a thumb of 16 dp off / 24 dp on centred half a track height
    from either end, colours per the theme (off: surface3 / text2 thumb; on: accent / onAccent; disabled on:
    text3 at .35 / text2; disabled off: surface3 / text3). Before: one 24 dp thumb in both states, no outline, the
    disabled track composited at a made-up alpha over the ground.
  - Other styles draw the track as a `Toggle` surface and a 24 dp `Control` thumb (`_KitStyledSwitch`), through
    `kitSurfaceLook`, so the row never reflows between styles.
  - Row geometry as `KitRow`: 56 dp one line / 64 dp two lines, the 48 dp switch reaching into the words' room,
    end padding space1 + the switch's own space2 (so the track ends on the 16 dp rail, as golden x = 380 shows), a
    2 dp `rowLineGap` under the title, title up to 2 lines, supporting 2 lines by default (was 3).
  - Disabled: nothing is dimmed (`kit_row_parts.dart:270-271`); the title keeps text1 (was text3).
  - Risk (`KitRisk`, `KitUntil`, new `riskSpec` parameter; the older `risk: String` still works with one duration):
    the step under the row states the scope, then one full-width secondary button per duration ("Until I turn it
    off", "For this conversation", "For an hour"; "Turn on" when only one), then "Not now"; space3 under the scope,
    space2 between buttons, space3 under the step, gutter on both sides. Before: a disabled "Turn on" and "Not now"
    side by side. While on, the supporting line reads "scope · duration · own line" and the row contributes a
    `RiskySwitch` status ("Turn off") to the slot above. The step folds when the switch turns on elsewhere.
  - Locked: the lock and "Always included" now end on the row's rail (`RowLockedValue` lost its extra space3 end).
- **KitExpandRow** (`kit_row_parts.dart:856-999`): header as a KitRow (24 dp lead glyph, 56/64 dp, title 1 line and
  supporting 1 line by default, as Flutter), the chevron in a 48 dp box with its ink on the 12 dp trailing rail
  (`KitOptical.endPad`, caret-down ink .15625), turning half a turn on the STANDARD spec; a new overload takes
  `supportingMaxLines` and `bare` (children revealed with no padding, as Flutter). Reduced motion: instant.
- **KitRowParts.kt**: `RowLockedValue` padding (above).

### part-kit_row_group_seam (KitRowGroup)

`kitRowGroupSeam(...)` ports `_KitGroupSeam`'s rule: an unlabelled group with a framed panel right under another
group keeps space3. Compose cannot see a previous sibling (Flutter walks the render tree), so the caller says it:
new `afterGroup` parameter (default false). "Framed" is approximated by "the style does not run lists flat"; Flutter
asks whether the panel's decoration has an edge or a shadow (`_frames`).

### part-kit_status_slot (KitStatusSlot)

`kitStatusShown(...)` is the slot's pick (`kit_status_slot.dart:143-154`), with Flutter's `omit` set (new overload
`KitStatusSlot(status, omit, content)`).

## Folded gaps (part-kit_row_value, folded into KitRowParts.kt; and parts of this order's own files not ported)

- `KitRowValue.count` (the count pill, `99+`) has no native variant.
- `KitRowValue` cross-fades a new value (`KitSwap`); native `RowValue` swaps instantly.
- `KitRowValue` puts the chevron's ink on the row's trailing rail with `KitOptical.endPad`; native `RowValue` sits
  inside `KitRow`'s 16 dp end padding (KitRow.kt, order 08, owns that padding: changing it here would move every
  order 08 golden).
- `KitRowValue` holds `KitMaxWidth.chip` (320); native relies on KitRow's half-window cap.
- `KitRowIcon` (current mark in an accent tile when lists are not flat) is `KitRowLeading.Glyph(current = true)`
  natively, accent glyph only, in every style.
- `KitChevron`'s optical rail: native `RowChevron` draws in KitRow's padding (order 08).
- `KitRowMenu` exists as `KitRowTrailing.Menu` (KitRow.kt, order 08).
- `KitStatusLineSlot.compact` and the short-window compact connection line need `KitStatus.compact()`, which
  KitStatus.kt (not in this order) lacks. The 40 % max height for a slot with a child is not ported (the native
  slot hands `shown` to its caller, who places the line).
- `_KitStyledSwitch` focus ring (`_KitSwitchFocusPainter`): native focus lands on the row (the switch is
  decorative), so the row's focus ring shows instead.
- The `quiet` switch of a row that opens on a tap (`onOpen`) is in `kitSwitchColors`/`KitSwitchTrack(quiet)`, but
  `KitOpenSwitchRow.kt` (not in this order) does not pass it yet.

## Gate design note (matrix caps)

`FlutterGoldenGate.matrix` measures one ink width per part and compares it to every cap class of the part. For
part-kit_row_parts the manifest lists list classes (KitSwitchRow, KitExpandRow: 960) and glyph classes
(KitChevron, KitRowIcon, KitRowMenu: 96) together, so a scene of rows can pass the list caps but never the glyph
caps. Needs a lead decision (per-class content, or measuring each class by tag); not worked around here.

## On-device checks for the coordinator

1. Settings with a switch row (e.g. Sounds): off shows a small dark thumb in a hair-outlined light track; tap the
   row: the thumb slides and grows to a white 24 dp thumb on the accent track within ~200 ms; with
   Settings › Text & motion › reduced motion, it jumps.
2. A disabled switch row: the title stays full ink; the reason is the supporting line; the switch rests dim.
3. A risky switch (auto-approve): tap: the step unfolds under the row with the scope sentence, three full-width
   duration buttons and "Not now"; pick one: the switch turns on, the step folds, the supporting line reads
   "scope · duration", and the screen's status line shows "Auto-approve is on" with "Turn off".
4. An expand row (Settings › built-in plugins, or any "More options"): the caret ends level with the chevrons of
   the rows around it; tap: it turns up and the rows unfold; RTL: the caret sits on the left rail.
5. A locked row ("Always included"): the words end on the same rail as a switch track.
