## Smoothness

- Each look draws in `KitLiveLayer`: its own `graphicsLayer`, drawn with `drawWithCache`/`onDrawBehind`; the clock's
  seconds are read only in the draw phase. `KitLiveBackgroundLooksTest.aTickRedrawsTheLookAndNeverThePage`: three
  seconds of ticks move the look and leave the page's composition and draw counts unchanged (with the layer removed,
  the page redrew 38 times).
- `KitLiveClock`: 12 ticks a second from `withFrameNanos` plus `delay` to the next tick, never faster (a 60 Hz run of
  frames gives 12 ticks); a late frame catches up at most 3 ticks; rest after 30 s without a touch, resume on the next
  touch where it stopped (`KitLiveClockRulesTest`).
- No allocation per frame: paths are rewound, paints and shaders kept in the look's memory; the radar turns its beam
  with the canvas instead of a new gradient; silk rebuilds a ribbon's gradient only when its band or colour changes
  (only while a reply runs); topo's field arrays are reused.
- Topo's field is computed separably (`LiveFbmGrid`: lattice hashes, column and row fades once per pass), bit for bit
  the values of `fbm` (`theSeparableFbmIsBitForBitTheSame`), and the points between contour lines take a per-row
  constant (`thePreparedPassGivesTheSameColoursAsPointByPoint`). Flutter's two triangles a cell are sent as one strip
  a row: the same triangles, a third of the indices.
- No AGSL: none of these four looks needed it to meet the goldens and the budget.

### Frame cost (`KitLiveFrameCostTest`, 1080x2400 px at density 2.625, medians of 120 frames after 30 warm-up)

Desktop JVM on an Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz (4 cores), Skia (Skiko). "record" is the look's `paint` into a display list
(the UI thread's work on a tick); "raster" is Skia drawing that frame on the CPU (an upper bound: the phone draws it on
the GPU); bytes are JVM heap per frame, the `empty` row being the harness's own.

| oscilloscope | 0.092 | 0.153 | 4.2 | 1208 |
| radar | 0.087 | 0.133 | 49.0 | 1000 |
| silk | 0.098 | 0.171 | 20.3 | 2040 |

## Folded gaps

- The "reply running" brighten (`KitLiveBackground.running`, 600 ms lift) has no native signal yet: looks draw idle
  (`running = 0`). Needs the composer's running state wired into the kit.
- `wantsTextHalo` / `KitTextHalo`: none of this order's looks ask for it; the background does not draw a halo yet.
- The preview tile does not pause while scrolled off screen (Flutter's `TickerMode(enabled: _visible)`).
- Keyboard input does not wake a resting clock (touch does).
- Order 22's `kit_live_look_preview_on_*` goldens: the native tile now has an "on" state, but its gate case breaks the
  layout guard's tablet control cap exactly as the already-baselined "Off" tile does
  (`config/layout/baseline/ui-kit.tsv:5-6`); the baseline may only shrink, so no gate case was added. The lead decides.
