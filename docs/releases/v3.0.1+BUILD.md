# OpenCode Mobile 3.0.1+BUILD

**The app could close by itself when you disconnected from, switched, saved, removed or tested a server. That is fixed.**

This is a fix-and-polish update to 3.0.0. It installs over 3.0.0 (and over 2.1.5) in place and keeps your data. It brings a new chat page, the Enter key you asked for, replies that no longer stay "running", plain words when a sign-in has expired, and Japanese. Everything below is what you will notice.

## Crashes and stalls

- The app could close by itself when you disconnected from, switched, saved, removed or tested a server. It no longer does.
- A reply no longer stays "running" after the connection drops and comes back. The app asks the server once what became of the reply and, if it has finished, shows it and stops waiting. If it is still working, the app keeps waiting.
- While a reply is thinking and the connection is alive, the "Still thinking" line keeps moving and counts the time ("Still thinking · 1 min 12 s"), with Stop beside it. After a few minutes it adds a calm note that some models think quietly for a long while. The grey, still line now appears only when the connection or the helper was really lost. With Motion off the time still counts and nothing sweeps.
- On a clean phone, the first set-up no longer fails at "Start OpenCode". It now finishes and the first free-model message is answered.
- A conversation that the agents' helper lists late now opens instead of vanishing.
- Opening New conversation while the phone's server is still starting waits, says "Getting ready" and loads by itself; it no longer says "couldn't be read" and asks you to try again. If the server truly does not start in time, you get plain words and Try again.

## The chat page

- **A new chat page.** The header floats as one rounded bar with the title and, under it, the project and place. The message box floats over the conversation, which now scrolls behind both and fades softly at their ends, with text flowing around them and no hard cuts. The jump-to-latest button is a round arrow above the box. The box's chips fit at 360 dp: the approval shield gives way to a shield alone when both chips cannot fit, and the model name keeps its place on one line.
- **Enter makes a new line.** The soft keyboard's Enter key now starts a new line in the message box instead of sending. Send is the Send button (a physical keyboard's Enter still sends).
- An approval you haven't answered stays on screen when you send another message; before, it could fold away and leave the agent waiting.
- A message you send while the agent is still working is kept if the app closes before it goes out; it comes back for you to review, and it's never sent twice.
- A step the agent stopped on before you answered no longer says it ran.
- Voice conversation now starts from the mic's row.
- Dictation now happens inside the message box itself. The separate recorder box above it is gone.
- A silent recording no longer types "[no audio]" or anything like it, and sends nothing.
- The model and its effort are said once ("Gemini 3.8 Flash (High)", not twice). An Antigravity conversation offers only the efforts its model really has.
- In the model sheet, Thinking sits at the top and stays put while the list scrolls. Its levels fit whole at 360 dp. A model without levels shows no Thinking row.
- Headings in a reply are sized by level, with room above them.
- Back returns to the exact line you were reading, also when you reopen the conversation from Home, All conversations, Search or the edge light. A reply notification still opens at the newest line.
- Plan mode on Codex on your phone now holds: it stops every edit and command and says "Stopped by Plan" on the step. Before, Codex still asked first.
- Haptics carry meaning: approving, declining, stopping and cancelling an agent card feel firmer than a tab switch or a copy.

## Signing in

- Signing in to Claude Code from the app now confirms by itself, even while a chat keeps Claude Code open. You no longer have to leave and come back to see that it worked.
- An expired sign-in now says so, once and plainly. For example: "Claude Code needs you to sign in again" with a "Sign in again" button. Your message stays in the box and nothing is sent twice after you sign in. The raw text is under Details. One rule reads it everywhere (the message, the banner, the Agents page, Home).
- After you sign in again, an earlier stopped turn no longer offers "Sign in again".
- The notification for a stopped turn says which agent needs you to sign in again.
- When a reply fails because the sign-in expired, the failed turn says so in one sentence with one "Sign in again" button that opens the sign-in. It no longer adds "Open providers" or "Choose another model", which could not help.
- Usage (Limits and Plans) now reads the same sign-in state as the Agents page. It says "Sign-in not confirmed" with "Check Claude Code again" when the check had no answer, and never "Not signed in" on a guess. A weekly limit reads as a limit.
- On This phone, a failure to reach an agent names the phone's agents, not "your computer".

## Downloads

- The two big first-open downloads (OpenCode about 130 MB, the agents' helper about 330 MB) wait for Wi-Fi and now say so calmly in Home's Recent label: "OpenCode update waiting for Wi-Fi · about 130 MB". Nothing downloads on mobile data unless you tap "Download on mobile data" and confirm. A VPN is judged by the network under it: a VPN over Wi-Fi counts as Wi-Fi, a VPN over mobile data counts as mobile data.
- While one runs, Home shows its progress ("Updating OpenCode · 40%"). If it fails, it says so and offers Try again.

## Time and languages

- **Time words in every language.** "5 minutes ago", "Resets in 2 hours" and "1 min 12 s" are now written by one rule per language, with the right plural forms (Arabic six forms, Russian four). Home, conversations, usage and limits, the phone, notifications, the edge light and the widgets all use it, and notifications and widgets now follow all offered languages. Under a minute reads as a few seconds, and the usage widget no longer says "Resets in now".
- **Japanese is complete** and is now offered in the language list. Its first-launch tour names the styles as the picker does.
- **Chinese is complete** (Simplified).

## Privacy and security

- **Our own usage server, same switch.** If you turn on usage and app health, the reports go to Google and also to our own Umami server hosted with Railway, in the same few fixed event words and ranges. It stores no IP address or personal identifier, and the anonymous visitor mark it keeps changes every month. It is the same single opt-in: switch it off and neither receives anything, and reports to our server that could not be sent are discarded after three days. "What we collect" and the privacy policy name both recipients in every language.
- With usage reports on (they are off unless you turn them on), the app now also sends our own server a random install ID, the phone's model and a few ranges (storage, memory, text size, screen width, agents used), to count installs and find faults. Switching usage reports off deletes the install ID. "What we collect" lists every field.
- The privacy page now names what Google Analytics collects itself.
- Reports now carry the build number so a problem can be tied to a build, and the app tells us when it was closed by Android (low memory, a crash, a stuck screen) as one fixed word, never any content.
- The notices now say what is true: "Your conversations and code go only to the servers you choose." They no longer claim the app sends nothing else. The site, the store text and the README say the same.
- What we collect is easier to read: no line is cut at 360 dp.
- Anything you copy from a password or key field, a sign-in or pairing code, error details or a log is marked sensitive, so Android 13 and later keeps it out of the clipboard preview and the keyboard's suggestions. Copying a reply, a message or a code block works as before and shows as usual.
- The security checks now run in every release check, and a release is refused without them.

## Phone, schedules and agents

- The agents move to newer versions: Claude Code 2.1.327, Codex 0.170.0, Antigravity 1.5.11, OpenCode 2.3.2, Paseo 0.16.1 and Node 24.22.3.
- Antigravity's mark now shows in its own colours.
- OpenCode now updates itself on the first run after an app update; before, it could stay behind. If an update has to wait for a reply to finish, Home says so.
- Updates no longer hang. When the phone is short of space, an update says so with the amount, and one update no longer blocks another. A failed agent update ends its wait and says why.
- A sub-agent that waits for your approval now shows "Needs you" on its parent conversation on Home and in the list. The parent's step reads "Waiting for you" and opens by itself.
- "Get latest" and the automatic update check use your GitHub sign-in in the built-in Linux, as push already does, so private repositories work. Thanks to AriEnzo (#149).
- A calm light runs round Home's "+" while a new conversation is being prepared.
- Scheduled prompts you made in the old app (2.1.5) run after the update without opening and saving them again, including the one on This phone.
- One name, "This phone", on Home and everywhere else; a "This device (Termux)" profile from the old app is carried over under it.
- A Gemini CLI left over from earlier is shown as "No longer offered on this phone", with only Remove. After removing an agent, the freed size reads "Freed 97 MB" instead of a raw number of bytes.
- The phone's Linux helper now starts reliably, even if an earlier step failed. An OpenCode update no longer reports success while the old version still runs. A failed install says so in plain words, with the technical text under Details. Suggestion chips never lose what they learned: if their saved list cannot be read, it is kept aside rather than overwritten.
- The agents' helper now starts when you open a conversation with a phone agent, even if that agent's source is hidden.
- The "Keep the server running?" question now appears only when the app is quiet (on Home or All conversations, no sheet open, no reply running), and once it is on screen it stays until you answer.
- Settings search now finds the pages and switches inside Privacy and data: What we collect, the privacy policy, usage and app health, crash reports.

## Looks and small screens

- Waiting now has its own small marks: starting, warming up, downloading, sending. Choose Ink or Colour in Settings › Appearance, under Waiting marks. They stand still with Motion off.
- Large text settings on Android 14 and later now lay out as intended: the app follows the text size you chose, so names are no longer cut or broken in the middle of a word. Background tiles take fewer columns at large text.
- On Android 14 and older, the chat no longer stops at a flat bar above the navigation area.
- Glass now keeps its look when Motion is off: reduced motion no longer turns Glass into a flat style.
- Every part of the app wears the style you chose. Cards, sheets, toasts, the undo bar and chips no longer draw a Solid face in the middle of Glass, Brutal or Paper.
- Nothing is cut at 360 dp: the New conversation folder line, the last-run line of scheduled prompts, the Agents tile action ("Check again"), tab labels and Home's filter chips, which continue on a second line when they cannot fit. At large text, agent tiles go to one column.

## Still rough

**Sub-agents.** Following the helpers a conversation starts (their own pages, the Subagents list, and how the edge dock shows them) still needs a lot of work: their state and running time can be out of date, and these pages are not easy to read yet. Reworking them is the first job of the next update.

## Known in this build

- With a live background switched on, opening or closing New conversation can flash dark. The default background is off.

## Reporting a problem

Open Settings › Help › About for the build number, then tell us what you tapped and what you saw. Settings › Report a problem keeps the recent errors and lets you share them. Say which screen you were on, whether a phone agent or a computer was involved, and whether it happened right after the update.
