# OpenCode Mobile 3.0.0+10020

The app rebuilt as a native Android app: the same servers, conversations and projects, smoother to use, with new looks. It installs over 2.1.5 and keeps your data.

Change class: breaking

**Migration.** Installs in place over 2.1.5. Your servers and their passwords, conversations, projects, the Linux on this phone, settings, language and your analytics choices are carried over on the first start; nothing needs doing. Two things to know before you update:

- **Computers need OpenCode 2.** The app no longer connects to an OpenCode 1 server; it tells you so and shows the update command. "This phone" updates its own OpenCode.
- **64-bit phones only.** This release has no build for 32-bit ARM phones. Android refuses the update there and 2.1.5 stays installed.

## What changed for you

- **A native app.** Smoother scrolling, typing and page changes, and it opens on what it last showed.
- **New looks.** Six styles including **Glass**, and a new **Appearance** page with a live preview.
- **Chat.** The keyboard stays up when a notice or a tip appears.
- **Sharing.** Share pictures and files from other apps into a new conversation.
- **This phone.** OpenCode 2.2.6 on the phone, with a clear first step after setup.

## Technical changes

### Added
- Native Kotlin and Compose app in place of the Flutter app, same package and signer, upgrade in place with data migration.
- Styles (Solid, Glass, Soft, Brutal, Paper, Signal), the Appearance page with tabs and a live preview, live background looks.
- Screens open on their last known content while they refresh.
- Share pictures and files into a new conversation's draft.
- Analytics, only for people who agreed: a first-run funnel with time ranges, visit depth, finer slow-frame ranges with frozen frames, counts of model, agent, camera, Git and review actions, and which kind of starter was tapped (never its text). All listed under Settings > Privacy and data > What we collect.
- Help > Tips to replay any tip.

### Changed
- Computer servers must run OpenCode 2. OpenCode 1 servers are refused with the update command.
- The phone's own server is OpenCode 2.2.6; Paseo 0.15.3.
- Builds for arm64-v8a and x86_64 only.
- The pattern background looks (halftone, blueprint, circuit, honeycomb, brushed metal) are no longer offered; a saved one still shows.
- The offline demo uses the app's own request card and message box.

### Fixed
- A status line or a tip arriving while you type no longer closes the keyboard or makes the conversation jump.
- After setting up This phone, "New conversation" could sit below the screen under a notice.
- The false "Android closed the app" notice after you closed it yourself.
- A folder without Git offers Initialize instead of an error; Open on a folder that cannot be a project says why.

## Known limits
- Smoothness was measured on emulators and in code, not yet on a range of real phones.
- First published as a pre-release: neither 2.1.5 nor this app offers a pre-release as an update, so install it by hand from this page until it is promoted.
- The new What we collect lines in Spanish, French, Indonesian, Portuguese, Russian and Vietnamese are machine translated and not yet reviewed.
