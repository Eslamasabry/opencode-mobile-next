# OpenCode Mobile 3.0.3+10160

This update replaces 3.0.2 in place and keeps your data. It stops the app from running out of memory when it is left open for hours, keeps a running reply still between its steps, brings a conversation with your computer back by itself, and offers newer versions of Codex and Antigravity. The app asks before it downloads them.

## In short

- **The app no longer closes when left open for hours.** With OpenCode running on this phone, the app could run out of memory after sitting idle. It now measures the phone's Ubuntu folder only while you look at it.
- **A running reply keeps still.** The status line, the word Working, the step marks and the rows stay where they are between steps.
- **Conversations with your computer come back by themselves**, and **Try again** reconnects a computer that went away.
- **Get latest** works on a branch the remote already has.
- **The "go to the newest" button no longer covers the text**, and Stop, the Agents page, the first setup screen and dark mode look cleaner.
- **Newer agents on your phone**: Codex and Antigravity. The app asks before it downloads them.

## Agents on this phone

If you use coding agents on your phone, this update offers newer versions of these. The app asks before it downloads them and shows the size; on mobile data it says so and waits for your yes. Sizes are for a phone (download, then space taken once installed).

- Codex 0.172.0: about 202 MB to download, 522 MB installed.
- Antigravity 1.5.17: about 94 MB to download, 261 MB installed. Long replies no longer fail when your Google sign-in passes its hour, and the agent no longer waits a minute when a helper agent asks for your approval.

Claude Code, OpenCode, the agents' helper and the other agents keep the versions they had in 3.0.2.

## Privacy

- When OpenCode on this phone fails to start, the app now keeps what kind of failure it was (for example, the port is busy or the start was refused), also after a restart, and no longer reports the same old failure again. If you turned usage reports on, they carry that kind of failure and nothing else about it. The privacy policy and What we collect say so in every language.

## Fixed: running out of memory

- The app could run out of memory and close after it was left open for hours while OpenCode ran on this phone. It measured the size of the phone's Ubuntu folder every few minutes in the background, sometimes twice at once. Now it measures it only while the This phone page is open, and never twice at once.
- A conversation that sits open with nothing new no longer redraws all its steps every second. Only the times change, and only while the conversation is on screen.

## Fixed: conversations

- While a reply runs, the status line, the word Working, the step marks and the rows stay still between steps instead of jumping or blinking.
- A conversation with your computer that lost its connection tries again by itself and shows a countdown, without a tap and without flashing Loading. It waits while the app is hidden or the phone is offline.
- Try again in a conversation now reconnects a computer that went away. It shows Connecting while it waits and the real reason if the computer stays away.
- When you come back to a conversation, a command that is still running shows as soon as the connection returns.
- The round "go to the newest" button no longer sits over the text: it takes the place of the live line at the bottom.
- Stop is a clear pill that turns amber when a stop takes long, and the approval shield keeps its green tint while it is on.

## Fixed: Git

- Get latest on a branch that was never linked now works when the remote already has a branch with the same name: the app links them and gets the latest. If the remote has no such branch, it still asks you to push the branch first.

## Fixed: other pages

- In dark mode, the phone's top and bottom bars stay dark after you rotate the phone, and menus, tips and full-page views use them too.
- The Agents page shows its cards two side by side on a narrow phone, and their buttons wrap on whole words at large text sizes instead of being cut. The phone's line no longer shows its chip type and Android version, a computer shows its folder's name, and Add a computer takes the full width.
- The first setup screen no longer shows the developer bug icon (Report a problem stays in Settings › Help), and every list marks your choice with the same check mark.
- The one-time card that asked you to star the project is gone. Instead, the bottom line of your first good reply asks once "Enjoying it?".

## Known in this version

These are known and planned for a later update.

- In a long reply, earlier parts can still shift while new steps arrive, and the page can move when you have scrolled up. This part is still being rebuilt.
- When a conversation opens, its page can shift once, for a single frame.
- On the floating conversation page, there is a gap above the message box.
- A task running in the background shows no start and end time yet, and its status check can read "Other".
- The first message in a new conversation can fail with "model not found" when the model you chose is no longer offered. Pick another model and send it again.
- The layout for tablets and other wide screens is not in this release.
